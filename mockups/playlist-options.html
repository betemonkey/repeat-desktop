On repeat · playlists

How a playlist would fit

Nothing new to learn. The Loop card gains a third mode, the Recent card gets a Playlists tab, and the mini window gets previous/next and a "3 / 12" position. Counts stay per video: each song in a playlist adds to its own lifetime count and to the all-videos total, as today.

P1 · Paste a YouTube playlist

Paste a playlist link (anything with list=) into the same box. The YouTube player plays the list itself, and "Loop: playlist" starts it again from the top after the last song.

P2 · Your own playlists in the app

Make lists from anything you've played or found: a "+" on each Recent or search row adds it to a list. Lists are saved on this PC next to your counts. You can drag to reorder, play in order or shuffled, and remove songs (with Undo, like Recent).

Previous and next appear beside play/pause only when a playlist is playing. The strip shows the position. With one video looping, the mini window stays exactly as it is now.

To decide

  • P1, P2 or both? My pick is both, P1 first. P1 is the smaller step because YouTube's player already plays and loops lists. P2 is the bigger one: storage, the "Add to…" menu, drag to reorder, shuffle.
  • What counts as a listen in a playlist? Today a loop counts when a video reaches its end. In a playlist the player moves straight to the next song, so the app would count a song when it plays to its end, not when you skip it. That matches the current rule.
  • Not checked yet: exactly which events the YouTube player sends between playlist songs. I'd test that first, because the counting depends on it.
