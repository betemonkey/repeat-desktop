How it would work

  • App: a small C# program (.NET 9, already on this PC) with Microsoft's WebView2, the Edge engine Windows 11 ships with (version 154 here). It needs one NuGet package, Microsoft.Web.WebView2. It builds to one .exe.
  • Page: it loads /repeat?app=1. In app mode the page hides its header and search, and asks the app to resize the window when you switch between full and mini or fold and unfold. Full mode is the normal layout D page, search and all. The browser version of /repeat stays as it is.
  • Progress and counts: one player, so nothing is handed over. The thin gold line is the video's progress, shown even when folded.
  • Autoplay: WebView2 accepts Chromium start-up flags, so the app should be able to allow sound without a first click and start on your last video right away (to be confirmed when built).
  • Remembers: window position, size, mini/full and folded state, per PC.
  • Fold: hides the video and keeps title, play/pause, progress and counters. That's your call against YouTube's 200 px rule. I'll check the music keeps playing while it's hidden before calling it done.
