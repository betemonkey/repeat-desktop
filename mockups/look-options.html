To know before picking C1 or C2

  • Real sound: the bars would follow the music for real, with the same capture as the Stream Deck knob. It captures only On repeat's own audio, never other apps.
  • Why the page can't do it itself: the sound comes from YouTube's player inside its own protected frame, so the page can't measure it. The app would measure it and send the levels to the page about 30 times a second.
  • Paused or muted: the bars drop flat and dim.
